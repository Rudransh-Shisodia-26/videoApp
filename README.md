**Video Conferencing Application**

A web-based video conferencing application that enables users to host and join virtual meetings with high-quality audio and video. Built with a focus on ease of use and reliability, this application serves as a powerful tool for remote communication, team collaboration, and online learning.

**Features**

**Video Conferencing**: High-quality video and audio capabilities for seamless communication.

**Real-Time Chat**: In-meeting chat functionality for participants to exchange messages and share links.

**Screen Sharing**: Users can share their screens to present documents, applications, or presentations.

**Meeting Scheduling**: Option to schedule meetings in advance and generate unique meeting links.

**User Authentication**: Secure user registration and login to manage meetings and participants.

**Virtual Backgrounds**: Users can customize their backgrounds for a more professional appearance.

**Recording**: Ability to record meetings for later playback (if implemented).

**Technologies Used**

**Frontend**: React.js, WebRTC, Material-UI

**Backend**: Node.js, Express.js

**Real-Time Communication**: Socket.IO

**Database**: MongoDB
